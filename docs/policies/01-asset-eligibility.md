# Asset Eligibility Policy

Owner: IT Infrastructure · Version 3.1 · Effective 2025-04-01

## Standard allocation by role

Every full-time employee is entitled to one primary computing device. The
standard allocation is:

- **Engineering, Design**: one laptop (developer or design specification), one
  external monitor, one headset.
- **Operations, Support**: one laptop (standard specification), one external
  monitor. Barcode scanners and label printers are allocated per site rather
  than per person.
- **Field and logistics roles**: one mobile handset and one handheld scanner.
- **Managers and above**: standard allocation for their function, plus a mobile
  handset.

Contractors receive the standard allocation for their function but are not
eligible for a mobile handset unless their engagement is longer than six months.

## Second devices

A second laptop is not part of standard allocation. It requires written approval
from the employee's reporting manager and the Head of IT, and is granted only
for demonstrable need such as cross-platform testing or on-call duty. Requests
without both approvals are rejected at intake.

## Monitors and peripherals

Employees may hold up to two external monitors. Keyboards, mice, docking
stations and headsets are consumables and are not tracked individually in the
asset register; they do not require an asset code.

## Specification upgrades

An upgrade above the standard specification for a role requires manager approval
and a written justification describing the workload that needs it. Approved
upgrades are fulfilled from existing stock where a suitable unit is available
before any new purchase is raised.

## Eligibility during notice period

Employees who have given notice are not allocated new or replacement assets
except where a device has failed and the employee cannot work without it. In
that case a loaner unit is issued and must be returned on the last working day.
