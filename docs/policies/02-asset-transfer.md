# Asset Transfer and Reassignment Procedure

Owner: IT Infrastructure · Version 2.4 · Effective 2025-07-15

## When a transfer is required

An asset transfer must be recorded whenever custody of a tracked asset moves
from one employee to another. This includes internal moves between teams,
temporary handovers longer than ten working days, and any move between office
locations. Lending a device informally without recording the transfer leaves the
original holder accountable for it.

## Approval requirements

- **Within the same team**: the reporting manager of the team approves.
- **Between teams or departments**: both the current holder's manager and the
  receiving employee's manager approve.
- **Between office locations**: both managers approve, and IT Infrastructure
  arranges shipment and updates the asset location.
- **To or from a contractor**: Head of IT approval is required in addition to
  the managers.

Approvals must be recorded before the physical handover, not after.

## Condition check

The current holder and the receiving employee jointly confirm the condition of
the asset at handover. If the recorded condition changes as a result — for
example from Good to Fair — the asset register is updated at the same time as
the transfer. Any pre-existing damage must be noted at handover; damage found
later is attributed to the receiving employee.

## Data handling before transfer

Laptops and mobile handsets must be wiped and re-imaged before transfer to a new
holder. The outgoing holder is responsible for backing up their own work to
approved storage first. A device may not be transferred with the previous
holder's profile intact.

## Assets that cannot be transferred

Assets with status **In Repair** cannot be transferred until the repair is
closed and the status returns to Available or Assigned. Assets marked
**Retired** cannot be transferred at all; they are disposed of through the
e-waste process.

## Recording the transfer

The transfer is recorded in the asset register with the asset code, both
employees, the date, and the reason. The register is the system of record: if a
transfer is not recorded there, it did not happen for audit purposes.
