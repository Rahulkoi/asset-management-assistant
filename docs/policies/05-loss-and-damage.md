# Reporting Loss, Theft or Damage

Owner: IT Infrastructure · Version 2.2 · Effective 2025-06-01

## Report immediately

If a company asset is lost or stolen, it must be reported to IT Infrastructure
within **24 hours** of discovery. This deadline applies to any missing device —
a laptop left in a taxi, a stolen handset, or equipment that has simply gone
missing. Reporting late delays the remote wipe and increases the risk of data
exposure, and is treated as a separate matter from the loss itself.

Report damage as soon as it occurs, or at the latest at the end of the working
day.

## What to include in the report

- The asset code, if known. If not, describe the device and when it was issued.
- Where and when it was last seen.
- Whether the device was powered on and whether it was encrypted.
- For theft: whether a police report has been filed.

## Immediate actions by IT

On a loss or theft report, IT Infrastructure triggers a remote wipe, revokes the
device's access to company systems, and sets the asset status to Retired with a
note recording the circumstances. For a mobile handset, the SIM is suspended.

## Police report

Theft of an asset must be reported to the police and the reference number
provided to IT Infrastructure within five working days. Insurance claims cannot
proceed without it.

## Liability

Employees are not held personally liable for loss or damage that occurs in the
normal course of work. Recovery of cost may be considered only where there is
evidence of negligence — for example a device left unattended in a public place
or in a visible position in a parked vehicle — and requires Head of IT approval
and HR involvement.

Repeated incidents involving the same employee are reviewed with their manager.

## Replacement

A replacement is issued from spare stock at the employee's location where one is
available. Where none is available, a request is raised and the employee is
issued a loaner device in the interim. Replacement is not delayed pending the
outcome of an insurance claim.
