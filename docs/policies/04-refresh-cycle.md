# Hardware Refresh Cycle

Owner: IT Infrastructure · Version 2.0 · Effective 2025-04-01

## Refresh intervals

Assets are scheduled for refresh based on the purchase date recorded in the
asset register:

| Category | Refresh interval |
|---|---|
| Laptop | 4 years |
| Mobile handset | 3 years |
| Monitor | 6 years |
| Printer | 5 years |
| Scanner | 5 years |

An asset becomes **eligible** for refresh once it reaches the interval above. It
is not replaced automatically — eligibility opens a request, it does not
guarantee one.

## How refresh is prioritised

Each quarter IT Infrastructure produces a refresh list ordered by purchase date,
oldest first. Within the same quarter, priority goes to:

1. Assets in Poor condition, regardless of age.
2. Assets whose repair history shows two or more corrective visits in twelve
   months.
3. Assets held by employees in roles with the heaviest workloads for that
   hardware class.

## Early refresh

Refresh before the interval requires manager approval and a documented reason:
sustained performance problems, a role change requiring a different
specification, or repeated hardware faults. Preference for a newer model is not
a qualifying reason.

## What happens to the replaced asset

A replaced asset is assessed on return. Units in Good or Fair condition return
to spare stock as **Available** and are re-allocated, typically for lighter
workloads or as loaner units. Units in Poor condition, or beyond the AMC
exclusion age of five years, are marked **Retired** and disposed of through the
approved e-waste vendor.

## Location of refreshed stock

Refresh stock is held at the four primary sites — Bangalore, Pune, Mumbai and
Hyderabad. Employees at other locations are shipped their replacement, which
adds three to five working days to fulfilment.
