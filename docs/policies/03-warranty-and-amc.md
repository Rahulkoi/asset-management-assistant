# Warranty and AMC Coverage

Owner: IT Infrastructure · Version 1.9 · Effective 2025-02-01

## Standard warranty terms

- **Laptops**: three years manufacturer warranty from the purchase date,
  including on-site next-business-day support at Bangalore, Pune, Mumbai and
  Hyderabad. Chennai, Delhi and Kolkata are covered by carry-in support.
- **Monitors**: three years manufacturer warranty, carry-in at all locations.
- **Mobile handsets**: one year manufacturer warranty. Screen damage is not
  covered.
- **Printers and scanners**: one year manufacturer warranty, extended by the
  annual maintenance contract described below.

## Annual maintenance contract

Printers, scanners and other shared site equipment are covered by an AMC that
runs from 1 April to 31 March each year. The AMC covers preventive maintenance
twice a year, unlimited corrective visits, and consumable parts other than
toner and paper. Devices older than five years are excluded from AMC renewal and
are scheduled for replacement instead.

## Raising a warranty claim

Raise a ticket with IT Infrastructure quoting the asset code. IT verifies the
purchase date against the asset register and confirms coverage before contacting
the vendor. Employees must not contact the vendor directly — a claim raised
outside the process is not tracked and may void the on-site entitlement.

## While an asset is being repaired

The asset status is set to **In Repair** for the duration. An asset in this
state is not available for allocation or transfer. Where the repair is expected
to take more than two working days, a loaner device of equivalent specification
is issued from spare stock if one is available at that location.

## Out-of-warranty repairs

Repairs to assets outside warranty require manager approval where the quoted
cost exceeds 25% of the replacement cost of an equivalent new unit. Above that
threshold, replacement is normally the approved route.
