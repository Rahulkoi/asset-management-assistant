# Work-From-Home Equipment

Owner: IT Infrastructure · Version 1.6 · Effective 2025-05-01

## What may be taken home

An employee's allocated laptop, headset and mobile handset may be taken home as
a matter of course; no separate approval is needed. These remain company assets
and remain recorded against the employee in the asset register.

## Monitors and larger items

An external monitor may be taken home with manager approval. The asset location
in the register is updated to the employee's base office location with a
home-use note — it is not recorded as a separate site. Only one monitor per
employee may be taken home.

Desks, chairs and other furniture are not IT assets and are handled by Facilities.

## Employees who are fully remote

Fully remote employees receive the standard allocation for their role shipped to
their registered address. Their assets are recorded against their base office
location for reporting purposes, which is the office their reporting manager
belongs to.

## Insurance and care

Company assets used at home are covered by the company policy provided
reasonable care is taken. Assets must not be used by anyone other than the
employee, including family members, and must not be taken outside the country
without written approval from IT Infrastructure — an unapproved international
move voids cover.

## Returning equipment for on-site work

Employees moving from remote to on-site working return home-use monitors to
their office within two weeks of the change. The register is updated at the
point of return.

## Internet and power costs

Broadband and electricity costs are handled through the remote-working allowance
administered by HR and are outside the scope of this policy.
